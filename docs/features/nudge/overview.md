# Overview of Nudge Feature

The Nudge feature is designed to enhance communication and task completion efficiency within the organization. Its main purpose is to gently push tasks forward when they are at risk of delay, ensuring that all stakeholders are reminded and prompted for actions timely.

## Happy Path Steps
- **Scan**: Identify tasks that are potentially delayed or require attention.
- **Send**: Dispatch reminders or notifications to the relevant stakeholders.
- **Escalate**: If no action is taken, escalate the task to higher-ups to ensure it is addressed.
